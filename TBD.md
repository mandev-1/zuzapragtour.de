This is a solid, professional landing page. It feels trustworthy, which is the most important factor for a private tour guide. However, because this is your **"conversion page"** (where you want people to finally commit), there are several ways to reduce friction and increase the number of bookings you receive.

Here is a benchmark and a list of improvements:

### 1. The "Above the Fold" Experience (First Impression)
* **The Issue:** The headline "Tour buchen" (Book a Tour) is clear, but the page starts with a lot of empty white space. The actual form is a bit low on the screen.
* **Improvement:** Reduce the vertical padding at the top. Bring the "Buchungsanfrage" (Booking Request) box higher so it’s partially visible without scrolling.
* **The "Social Proof" Tweak:** Add a small badge or text right under the main title: *"Join 300+ happy travelers"* or show the 5-star TripAdvisor rating immediately.

### 2. Form Optimization (Reducing "Friction")
The form is the most important part of this page. Currently, it asks the user to do a lot of "thinking" in the "Ihre Anfrage" box.
* **Segment the Data:** Instead of asking people to type their group size and interests in a big text box, add two simple dropdowns or number selectors:
    * **Group Size:** (Dropdown: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5+)
    * **Tour Type:** (Dropdown: Old Town, Jewish Quarter, Hidden Gems, etc.)
* **Why?** The more a user has to type, the more likely they are to leave the page. Selecting from a list is "mentally cheaper."
* **Phone Number:** Make the phone number field optional (and state it). Some people are hesitant to give their number before a first contact.

### 3. Strengthening "The Closer" (The Left Column)
You have your contact info on the left, which is great, but it’s a bit passive.
* **Add your Face:** People are booking **you**, Zuzana. Put a small, friendly professional photo of yourself right there next to "Bereit zur Buchung?" It creates an immediate human connection.
* **Response Time Guarantee:** You mention "within 24 hours." If you can, change this to "usually within 2-3 hours" or "same day." In the travel industry, speed wins the booking.

### 4. Trust & Authority (Social Proof)
* **Move the Reviews Up:** You have the TripAdvisor 4.9 rating at the very bottom (the footer). This is too late. Move a "Featured Review" (a 1-sentence glowing quote from a real customer) directly under the booking form or in the left sidebar.
* **Security:** If you have an SSL certificate (which you do), adding a tiny "Secure Data" icon near the "Senden" button can subconsciously reassure users.

### 5. Mobile Experience
* **The WhatsApp Button:** On mobile, the WhatsApp button in the footer is your strongest tool. Ensure there is a **sticky "Book via WhatsApp" button** that follows the user as they scroll. Many tourists plan on the go and prefer WhatsApp over filling out a web form.

### 6. Technical/SEO Check
* **Page Speed:** Ensure the background images are compressed. If the form takes 3 seconds to load on a mobile connection in Prague's Old Town, the customer will go back to Google.
* **Confirmation Page:** What happens after they click "Senden"? Make sure it goes to a "Thank You" page that tells them exactly what happens next (e.g., *"I will check my calendar and email you a personal offer within 4 hours"*).

### Summary of Priorities:
1.  **Add your photo** next to the contact info.
2.  **Add dropdowns** for "Group Size" and "Tour Interest" to the form.
3.  **Move the TripAdvisor badge** from the footer to the top of the page.
4.  **Create a "Sticky" WhatsApp button** for mobile users.

By making these changes, you shift the page from being a "Contact Form" to being a "Booking Engine."
