**Location:** Place the portrait photo in this directory (`/public/images/`)

**Requirements:**
- **Filename:** `zuzana-portrait.jpg`
- **Recommended size:** 800x1000px (portrait orientation)
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High resolution, professional photo
- **File size:** Keep under 500KB for fast loading (compress if needed)

**How to add the image:**
1. Save Zuzana's portrait photo as `zuzana-portrait.jpg`

**Image optimization tips:**

- Ensure good lighting and professional appearance
- Square or portrait orientation works best
